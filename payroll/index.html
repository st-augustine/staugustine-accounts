Cloud Access

Single Login: Payroll uses the secure St. Augustine Accounts Supabase session. Only the Accountant account can open Payroll.

Cloud Data: Staff, settings and monthly payroll records are saved automatically to Supabase. The browser copy is only a local cache/fallback.

Tax Rule

Auto Tax = (Gross Salary − SSF/SSI) × Tax %, then the tax is rounded up to the next whole rupee. Example: Rs. 360 stays Rs. 360; Rs. 111.01 / 111.50 becomes Rs. 112. Manual tax is also saved as a whole rupee.
